



Welcome to my first “Test” Asset published on Unity. It’s something very simple, but I hope it will be helpful to everyone who uses it.



In the first folder of the Asset, you’ll find a directly usable prefab (Remember to assign your Layers in
“Layers That Are Considered Ground” inside Base Mechanics, in Module 04. They must match the Layers you
assign to your floors).

Even so, I strongly recommend that you take a look at the documentation, not necessarily the technical one,
but the one about how to use the Asset itself
(48A-00-FPC_CPP\English\FirstPersonController\04___Documentation\CPP FPC_How To_Use_V1.0_EN).
You’ll also see that I made a test scene and that, at the same time, you already have 2 Configs ready to try,
such as the Arcade one or the “Horror” one, to show the scope of this Asset.





This is the first Asset I have published, and it is actually part of a University Project,
but it is definitely the least ambitious one, as I have 2 others, probably around
100 times bigger and clearly much more personal, currently in development.

The reason for creating this Asset is not simply because “either I make it or I fail” hahaha, nor is it because I
think there is a lack of Assets of this type (FPC (CPP)), since I’ve also seen other free ones that are 48/10.
Rather, I wanted to take this University Project as an opportunity to create something that I could actually publish
and that, at the same time, could serve as a small showcase of what I am capable of making.

The idea is that, by the time I manage to finish my other 2 projects and publish them, you as the audience will be a
little more open to trusting me enough to purchase them (if they are useful to you, of course), knowing that what I
make has a lot of hard work and dedication behind it.
